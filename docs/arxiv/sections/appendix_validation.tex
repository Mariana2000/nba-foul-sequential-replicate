\label{app:validation}
\section{Foul-count validation}
\label{app:validation:detail}

We compare parsed foul counts from our pipeline to official box-score team foul totals (\texttt{PF}) for a sample of games. The validation script (\texttt{scripts/validate\_foul\_counts.py}) selects games from the games manifest, counts non-technical, non-flagrant fouls assigned to each team in \texttt{foul\_events.csv}, and compares to box-score team fouls.

Table~\ref{tab:validation} reports results for ten games from the opening week of the 2023--24 season (season end year 2024). In all ten games, parsed home and away foul counts equal official totals (difference~$=0$ for both teams).

This validation does not audit every game in the eight-season panel. It confirms that the foul-assignment logic reproduces official team foul totals on inspected games. Foul-\emph{type} labels from play-by-play may contain misclassification for specific events; heterogeneity results for residual ``personal'' fouls should be interpreted with that caveat.
