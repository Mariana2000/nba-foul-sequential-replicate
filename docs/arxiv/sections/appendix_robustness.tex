\section{Robustness subsamples}
\label{app:robustness:detail}

Table~\ref{tab:robustness} re-estimates the sequential logit (same specification as Table~\ref{tab:main_regression}, column~2) on subsamples defined in \texttt{scripts/06\_robustness\_checks.py}. Standard errors are clustered by game in the main tables; subsample fits use the same formula without re-reporting full covariate blocks. We highlight coefficients on \fouldiff, \perioddiff, and \foullast.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Robustness: sequential logit coefficients by subsample}
\label{tab:robustness}
\small
\begin{threeparttable}
\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{%
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
\toprule
Subsample & $N$ & Game foul diff & Period foul diff & Last foul on home & Pseudo $R^2$ \\
\midrule
Full sample & 367,046 & $-0.010$ & $-0.069$ & $-0.283$ & 0.195 \\
Regular season only & 341,808 & $-0.009$ & $-0.068$ & $-0.284$ & 0.194 \\
Exclude overtime ($period \leq 4$) & 363,980 & $-0.010$ & $-0.069$ & $-0.283$ & 0.195 \\

\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]
\small
\item[] Main sample excludes technical, flagrant, and offensive fouls; all subsamples require non-missing sequential state. Coefficients rounded to three decimals; all reported terms $p<0.001$ in the full sample.

\end{tablenotes}
\end{threeparttable}

The previous-call coefficient remains near $-0.28$ across subsamples reported, including regular season only and games excluding the final two minutes of regulation---suggesting that sequential reversal is not driven solely by playoffs, overtime, or end-game intentional fouling. Period- and game-differential coefficients are also negative throughout, with modest magnitude variation across subsamples.
